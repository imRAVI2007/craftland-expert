**Purpose:** Design or adjust a gameplay loop based on available Craftland project evidence.

**Required input:** Player goals, constraints, current gameplay sources/assets/data, target project, desired change, and validation scope.

**Prompt:**

> Help design this Craftland gameplay change from the supplied evidence. Map the current documented flow across managers, state controllers, CSV/config, prefab/component and HUD relationships. Do not promise undocumented engine capabilities or assume this extracted template represents all Craftland projects. Provide a small implementation plan and validation needs; do not modify anything unless asked.

**Expected output:** Current evidence map, proposed player flow, required data/assets/scripts, unknowns, and validation plan.
